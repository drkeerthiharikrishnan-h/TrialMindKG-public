Demo Question

"We are planning the registrational trial of TUB-040 in platinum-resistant ovarian cancer. Two NaPi2b ADCs failed before us. What killed them, do our phase 1b data show the same risks, and what should we change in patient selection, sample size and safety monitoring?"

The 5-Minute Live Demo Script

0:00 – 0:30
Show: The question, graph view (TUB-040, UpRi, lifastuzumab → NaPi2b)
Say: "Agents built this from registries and ChEMBL this morning. Termination reasons are verbatim from the registry."
0:30 – 1:30
Show: Panels a–c: lung expression, FAERS ILD%, internal ILD by exposure
Say: "Three public sources point at the lung. Internal data tell us whether it's happening in our patients."
1:30 – 2:30
Show: Panel d: ORR by NaPi2b TPS band, UPLIFT's cut-off marked
Say: "The system knows our TPS ≥75% is UPLIFT's definition. This shows what that cost us."
2:30 – 3:30
Show: Panel e: sample-size curve for N needed
Say: "UPLIFT would have needed N=588 to hit its true effect, not 141. Plan on the lower bound."
3:30 – 4:15
Show: Click any statement → provenance panel opens
Say: "Every sentence opens to the registry record, API record, or internal table it came from."
4:15 – 4:45
Show: Synthetic-data disclaimer
Say: "Plug in your real phase 1b tables. Same query runs on your data inside your AWS account."

The 7-Part Answer

① What Killed Them

[EXT] Registry termination reasons, UPLIFT's ORR vs. 12% bar, phase 1b replication failures, non-enrichment at TPS ≥75%

② Lung Safety

[EXT+KG] Expression (GTEx), genetics (Open Targets), payload-class safety (FAERS)

③ Internal ILD Risk

[INT] ILD events by Cycle 1 exposure tertile; compared with FAERS patterns

④ Biomarker Enrichment

[INT+EXT] Internal ORR by TPS band overlaid with UPLIFT's 15.6% and 12% bar

⑤ Replication Risk

[INT+EXT] Internal vs. UPLIFT cohort characteristics; prior FRα-ADC exposure flagged

⑥ Sample Size

[EXT+KG] Exact-binomial N under 3 scenarios; UPLIFT's under-power risk

⑦ 4 Recommendations

[KG] Patient selection, safety monitoring, sample size, biomarker validation

Frontend Architecture (Post-Kickoff)

Based on your PITCH_TUB040 and GETTING_STARTED vision.

Design Principles

  • You are the architect & validator. AI assistant writes code; you decide what to show and why.
  • Judges want the story. Public evidence → internal data → clinical recommendation.
  • Provenance is the value. Every number clicks through to its source: registry, API, internal table, computation.
  • Layer-aware. Statements tagged [EXT], [INT], [KG]; answers can be filtered to [EXT]-only before sharing.
  • Synthetic-safe. Internal numbers clearly labeled SYNTHETIC; no mistaking for real findings.

Frontend Components

Query Input

Plain-language question box. Checkboxes for layer filtering ([EXT] / [INT] / [KG]).

Interactive Graph

D3.js force-directed layout. Clickable nodes & edges. Provenance links on hover.

5 Evidence Panels

Lung expression (bar chart), FAERS (by payload), internal ILD (by exposure), ORR (by TPS), sample size (curve)

Provenance Drilling

Click any statement → side panel shows source record, exact value, retrieval timestamp, external link

Data Badges

[EXT] = public, [INT] = confidential, [KG] = inferred. Toggleable layer filtering.

Synthetic Disclaimer

Persistent banner on all internal-data panels. Links to code & data definitions.

Filter & Export

Export as: full report, public-only, slides, CSV. Filter by layer & specific trials/events.

Recommendations

4 protocol recommendations, each linked to its evidence trail. Clickable to expand.
