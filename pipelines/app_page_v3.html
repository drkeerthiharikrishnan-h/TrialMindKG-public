    Failed drug programs

    The trial layer holds failed drug programs, each with its trials, drugs, targets and the stated reason it failed. The cards below are the programs behind this role's worked examples; search for any other program or trial.

    Graph

    The trial graph of failed drug programs. Search above, then hover a node or a line for its card; click to pin it. "Add neighbours" in a pinned node card (or a double-click on the node) grows the graph. For the Pharos graph, open an entity card.
