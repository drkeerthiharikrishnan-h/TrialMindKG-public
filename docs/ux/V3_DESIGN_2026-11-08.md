# TrialMindKG page v3: decision-grade, nothing dropped (8 Nov 2026)

For the owner. `/v3` is a new page next to `/` and `/v2`. Neither of those changed: their files are untouched, and a test
checks that `/` and `/v2` still serve them byte for byte. Nothing was deployed. The hosted demo was not used.

**In one line:** v3 keeps every section of the first page. Every answer now opens with a bottom line. Every cited link is a
chip showing source, strength and verdict, and a tap opens the full record in a side panel. Every dot and line in a graph has
a hover card. A slider thins the graph by pruning confidence. Each prediction shows its paths hop by hop. Each role sees the
same content in its own order, with worked examples drawn from the failed programs.

Files (new unless marked):
- `pipelines/app_page_v3.html`: the page (1,496 lines, one file, no build step).
- `pipelines/answer_summary.py`: the bottom line. Fixed rules, no language model.
- `pipelines/persona_examples.py`: 19 worked examples, built from the trial layer when the page is served.
- `pipelines/app.py` (changed, additive only):
  - the `/v3` route;
  - a `summary` key on `/api/ask`, on the final event of `/api/ask_stream`, and on `/api/pharos/ask` and `/api/pharos/demo/{id}`;
  - `POST /api/summarize`.
- `tests/test_answer_summary.py` (14 tests), `tests/test_app_page_v3.py` (9 tests).
- `docs/ux/harness_v3/`: the journey harness (`bridge.py`, `run_page_v3.js`, `journeys.sh`, `summarize.py`), one JSON per
  journey, `_summary.json` and `persona_examples_check.json`.

## 1. The lens

The brief was to design as someone with 30 years across bench science, IT, AI and business, building this for real drug
discovery and development. Read that way, the page has one job: help a person decide something they can defend in a meeting.
That gives five principles.

1. **Cleaner is not leaner.** Nothing is deleted. Detail is folded (folds, tabs, side panel, pinned cards), never removed.
2. **Answer first, then the reasons, then the raw record.** Every answer starts with a bottom line: one sentence, 2-4 points,
   a confidence level with its reason, caveats, what would change it, and a next step. The evidence sits under it. The raw
   JSON is one tap further.
3. **Every sentence shows what it rests on.** Points carry row numbers, claim numbers ("2.3") and edge ids as buttons. A
   tap highlights the row or claim, or opens the record.
4. **Say how strong, in words.** Each link is labelled curated, experimental, statistical, text-mined, observed, unspecified
   or predicted, read from its BioLink knowledge level. Its verdict (kept or pruned), confidence and the pruning reasons
   appear in words.
5. **Abstain honestly.** An empty or weak answer says so and why: the problems list, or the reason the section is empty.
   It never shows an empty table.

## 4. The bottom line: how it is made

`pipelines/answer_summary.py`, called by the server, so the page invents nothing.

- **Inputs:** the answer itself. For graph answers, also the records of the cited edges, looked up by id in the graph that
  answered (`edge_facts`): source, layer, knowledge level, pruning verdict, confidence, reasons. Those are the same records
  the side panel shows.
- **Strength word:** read from the knowledge level.
  - `knowledge_assertion` → curated; or experimental, when the link's evidence kind is "binding or activity".
  - `statistical_association` → statistical.
  - `text_co_occurrence` → text-mined.
  - `not_provided` → unspecified.
  - `observation` → observed.
- **A row** is as strong as its weakest link, because a multi-hop row needs every hop.
- **Confidence, graph answers:**
  - **low** when more than half the rows are text-mined or predicted only, or cite a pruned link, or have no known level.
  - **high** when at least half the rows rest only on curated or measured links, from two or more sources, with no pruned link.
  - **moderate** otherwise.
  - The reason line gives the counts.
- **Confidence, reports:**
  - **low** when a claim met contradicting evidence, under a quarter of claims cite strong sources, or over half are
    hypothesis-generating.
  - **high** when at least 3 in 4 claims cite strong sources, at most a quarter are hypothesis-generating, and none has
    low id matching.
  - **moderate** otherwise.
- **Caveats** (each only when it applies):
  - text-mined-only rows;
  - a single source;
  - rows citing pruned links;
  - the query hit its LIMIT ("there may be more answers");
  - links not looked up;
  - a failed tested-question check;
  - the offline-model label;
  - hypothesis-generating claims;
  - low id matching;
  - empty report sections.
- **Tested on real answers** (local graph, 8 Nov):
  - PTGS2 → diseases: 50 rows, 5 sources, 40 text-mined only, so **low**.
  - Compounds binding PTGS2's protein: 20 of 20 rows measured, 2 sources, so **high**.
  - TULIP-1 report: 11 claims, all strong, 3 post-hoc, so **moderate**.
  - METREO: **high**.
  - Shared targets across failed programs: **high**, and says the trial layer carries no pruning verdicts.
  - The typed PTGS2 question with the offline model abstains, citing the problems list ("no query returned").

## 7. Server changes (additive)

- `GET /v3`: mirrors `home_v2`. It reads `app_page_v3.html` and puts the worked examples (JSON) in place of
  `/*__PERSONA_EXAMPLES__*/null`. If the trial graph fails to load, the page still loads, without examples.
- `summary` key on `/api/ask`, on the `report` event of `/api/ask_stream`, and on `/api/pharos/ask` and
  `/api/pharos/demo/{id}`. All other keys are unchanged; a test compares `demo/{id}` minus `summary` with `graph.demo()`.
  A summary failure returns `{error}` in that key and never breaks the answer.
- `POST /api/summarize {kind: pharos | query | trial, result}`: the same rules, for answers that carry no summary.
  - `/api/query` is left unchanged.
  - `/api/pharos/demo` (all five) is left unchanged, because `tests/test_index_graph.py` checks it for exact equality.
    The page asks `/api/summarize` for each of the five instead.

## 8. Data gaps found (shown as they are, nothing invented)

1. **No route lists all failed programs.** The panel shows the 12 programs behind the examples, plus a search over
   `/api/search`, filtered to programs and trials. A read-only `/api/programs` would let the page list them all at once.
2. **No structured failure category.** There is no efficacy / safety / futility field. The page shows the program's
   `what_failed` text as "Stated failure (program record)", and `why_stopped` from the registry. That field exists for 19 of
   45 trials. Phase comes from the trial's `phase` field.
3. **Three programs have no target** in the trial layer (P26 asundexian, P28 fordadistrogene movaparvovec, P34 delpacibart
   etedesiran), so their cards have no "Open <gene>" link. A program opened from search lists its drugs, not its targets.
   Targets show only on example cards, because the page would need a second hop.
4. **Trial-layer links carry no pruning verdict or confidence.** Their hover cards say so. The slider never hides them, and
   the bottom line says "None of the N links … carries a pruning verdict in this graph".
5. **Trial claims carry one strength per claim** (strong / moderate / limited), not a knowledge level per evidence item.
   Claim chips show source · evidence type · that strength.
6. **Potency is not in card rows.** Activity values (e.g. IC50 10.7) sit in each edge's measured values. They show in the
   side panel and in tested question 2's table, not in the card's binding rows.
7. **Picture edges do not carry verdict reasons.** The hover card fetches `/api/pharos/explain/<id>` on demand (cached).
   The EGFR picture holds 40 kept links and no pruned one: the card index picks kept links first for the picture.
8. **Predicted links have a model score, not a pruning confidence.** The slider always keeps them.
9. **Offline, every typed Pharos question abstains.** The offline querier has templates for the trial-graph schema only.
   With the hosted model they are answered, but that could not be checked from the sandbox. Pharos-side examples therefore
   run as cards or tested questions.
10. **No "medicinal chemist" or "portfolio" role exists.** Both lenses sit on the drug-development lead, whose role file
    says "medicinal_chemist (FACET) adapted to development decisions". Adding role files would change `/api/personas` for
    `/` and `/v2`, so none were added.
11. **Explorer nodes carry only id, label and category,** so their hover facts are the degree and a hint.
12. The card index holds at most 40 rows per evidence kind, as in v1.

## 10. What still needs a real browser (owner, laptop and phone)

jsdom does no layout, CSS or fonts, so all of this is unchecked:
- **Fonts and look.**
  - Do the Google Fonts load, and is the fallback acceptable offline?
  - Serif body text next to dense tables: readable?
  - Light and dark mode contrast of the muted text, the strength-word colours and the purple Final panel.
  - Visitors' browsers contact fonts.googleapis.com. If that is unwanted for privacy, self-host the fonts or drop them.
- **Hover cards.**
  - Placement near the window edges.
  - Moving between thin lines (there is a 14 px invisible hit line).
  - On a phone, a tap should pin the card as a bottom sheet above the nav bar, and ✕ or a tap elsewhere should close it.
  - On a phone, does the tap scroll the page by mistake?
- **Edge threshold.** Slider feel on touch, and whether greyed paths read as "below threshold".
- **Path panel.** The two-column layout at 980-1240 px and the single column below. Long hop chains wrapping.
- **Answer panels.** Is the 120 ms stagger pleasant or slow? The sticky pinned bottom line under the sticky header (64 px;
  58 px on phones): does it cover content?
- **Side panel.** The bottom sheet on iPhone (86 vh). The back stack inside it. Raw-record links open in a new tab.
- **Worked-example cards.** Density on a phone. Do the context blocks with ids (hover/never per role) read well?
- **The live stream.** Steps appear while a real trial report streams; in jsdom the stream was replayed in one piece.
- **Hosted model.** Typed Pharos questions with the hosted model: the bottom line on a real model answer.
- **Speed.** The time to first card on the hosted instance. Opening a card fetches the explain records of the first
  prediction's path hops (9 for EGFR), lazily and cached.

## 11. Behaviour changes against the first page (deliberate)

- Clicking a dot in a graph now **pins its hover card** with "Open its card" (or "Add its neighbours" in Explore). A
  **double-click** opens it directly. In v1 a single click opened the card at once.
- Evidence kinds are folds (the first two open) and their order follows the role. v1 showed every kind expanded, in a fixed
  order.
- The tested-question answer keeps all rows; the bottom line adds row refs.
- v3 keeps its settings under its own browser keys (`tmkg3_*`), so `/`, `/v2` and `/v3` do not interfere.
