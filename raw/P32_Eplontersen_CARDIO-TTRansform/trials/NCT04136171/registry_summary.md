## Arms and interventions
- **Eplontersen** (EXPERIMENTAL): Eplontersen by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks Interventions: Drug: Eplontersen
- **Placebo** (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR): Eplontersen-matching placebo by subcutaneous injection once every 4 weeks Interventions: Drug: Placebo

## Brief summary
To evaluate the efficacy of eplontersen compared to placebo in participants with ATTR-CM receiving available standard of care (SoC).
