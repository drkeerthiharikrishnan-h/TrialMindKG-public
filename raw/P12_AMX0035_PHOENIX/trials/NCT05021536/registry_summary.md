## Arms and interventions
- **Placebo** (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR): Placebo administered by mouth or via feeding tube for 48 weeks: once daily for first 3 weeks and then twice daily for remainder of study if participant tolerating Interventions: Other: Placebo
- **AMX0035** (EXPERIMENTAL): Placebo administered by mouth or via feeding tube for 48 weeks: once daily for first 3 weeks and then twice daily for remainder of study if participant tolerating Interventions: Drug: AMX0035

## Brief summary
The Phoenix Trial is a randomized double blind placebo controlled Phase III trial to evaluate the safety and efficacy of AMX0035 for treatment of ALS
