## Arms and interventions
- **Serelaxin (RLX030)** (EXPERIMENTAL): Participants received continuous intravenous infusion of serelaxin 30 ug/kg/day for 48 hours. Interventions: Drug: RLX030
- **Placebo** (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR): Participants received continuous intravenous infusion of matching placebo to serelaxin for 48 hours. Interventions: Drug: Placebo

## Brief summary
The purpose of the study was to evaluate the efficacy, safety and tolerability of intravenous infusion of serelaxin, when added to standard therapy, in acute heart failure (AHF) patients.
