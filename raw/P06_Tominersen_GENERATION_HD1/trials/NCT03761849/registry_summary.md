## Arms and interventions
- **RO7234292 Q8W** (EXPERIMENTAL): RO4234292 is administered intrathecally every 8 weeks. Interventions: Drug: RO7234292
- **RO7234292 Q16W** (EXPERIMENTAL): RO7234292 is administered intrathecally every 16 weeks. Participants in this arm will also receive placebo at alternate weeks to keep the blind. Interventions: Drug: RO7234292, Drug: Placebo
- **Placebo** (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR): Placebo will be administered every 8 weeks by IT injection. Interventions: Drug: Placebo

## Brief summary
This study will evaluate the efficacy, safety, and biomarker effects of RO7234292 (RG6042) compared with placebo in participants with manifest Huntington's disease (HD)
