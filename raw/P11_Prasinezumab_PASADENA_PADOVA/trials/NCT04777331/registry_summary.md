## Arms and interventions
- **Prasinezumab** (EXPERIMENTAL): Participants will receive an IV infusion of prasinezumab every 4 weeks (Q4W).

Participants will enter into the optional Open Label Extension (OLE) once the double-blind treatment period has completed. Interventions: Drug: Prasinezumab
- **Placebo** (PLACEBO_COMPARATOR): Participants will receive placebo as an IV infusion Q4W. Interventions: Drug: Placebo

## Brief summary
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study that will evaluate the efficacy and safety of intravenous (IV) prasinezumab versus placebo in participants with Early Parkinson's Disease (PD) who are on stable symptomatic PD medication.
